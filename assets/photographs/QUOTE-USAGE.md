# Quote hero photograph

The Quote hero reuses `originals/details.png`, the existing native 1536×1024 standalone original. It depicts a parcel on a digital scale being measured beside a clipboard in an illustrative Indian courier setting. The source was visually inspected before reuse; the full parcel, scale, tape and paperwork are retained.

This is an existing AI-generated illustrative photograph, **not actual VK AND COMPANY staff or premises**. No new image generation was performed for this update. Earlier generation provenance is recorded in `PROMPTS.md`.

The production component uses `Photograph name="details"` and existing local WebP variants at 480, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1536 pixels. The original and derivatives have not been enlarged. At the current Quote dimensions (423×282 desktop; 243×162 at ≤900px), native resolution is sufficient for the tested 2× desktop and 3× mobile displays. The alt text explicitly identifies the scene as illustrative.
